E-Bike Buddy (formerly Giant Buddy) is an independent ride computer for e-bikes with RideControl+ Bluetooth. Sample files (GPX and CSV) are in ride_2026-10-09.zip, attached to our previous Resolution Center reply.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
For owners of e-bikes with RideControl+. The bike's own display shows little and keeps no history. E-Bike Buddy turns iPhone and Apple Watch into a ride computer: live speed, motor and rider power, cadence, torque and battery; automatic ride recording even without getting the phone out of your pocket; cycling navigation with a battery prediction for the route; ride analysis and export; and bike health (odometer, battery, maintenance).

3. SETUP
- No account or login; no credentials are needed.
- Live data needs a compatible e-bike: Bike tab > Scan for Bikes, then tap the bike. Recording starts automatically when connected (Settings > Auto-Record on Connect).
- Without a bike:
  - Ride tab > magnifier: search a place or category (e.g. Bicycle Repair) and tap Navigate.
  - My Routes > + > Import GPX: import ride_2026-10-09.gpx from the attached zip (save the zip to Files and tap it to unzip), or Create Route.
  - History, Settings and the Apple Watch app.
- Crash detection test: Settings > Emergency Contacts & Crash Detection > Test Crash Alert. It shows the countdown only and sends nothing.
- Strava upload is optional and uses Strava's own OAuth sign-in (any Strava account).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple MapKit: maps, place search, directions.
- Apple WeatherKit: current weather and forecast.
- Apple CloudKit: syncs rides and routes to the user's private iCloud database.
- Apple HealthKit (optional): saves cycling workouts while excluding the energy the e-bike has put into the effort.
- WatchConnectivity: iPhone to Apple Watch app.
- Open-Meteo Elevation API (api.open-meteo.com): elevation for coordinates; no account, no personal identifiers.
- Strava API (optional): OAuth sign-in and upload of rides the user chooses.
- Messages: if a crash countdown isn't dismissed, the app opens the Messages composer prefilled with the location; the user must tap Send. Nothing is sent automatically.
- Bluetooth LE: only to the user's own e-bike.
There is no developer server, analytics, advertising or tracking.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in all regions. Map, search, directions and weather coverage follow Apple's services in each country.

6. GUIDELINE 4.1(a)
Following the 4.1(a) rejection, the app is renamed E-Bike Buddy in the App Store listing, on the Home Screen, in permission prompts and in the app itself; the app icon is redrawn without the word "Giant", and the screenshots are updated. The name, subtitle, keywords, promotional text and screenshots do not use "Giant". The app contains no Giant logos, artwork, software or other copyrighted material; it talks to the rider's own bike over Bluetooth. It is open source (MIT licence). Crash detection is a convenience feature, not an emergency service.

7. WEATHERKIT ATTRIBUTION (GUIDELINE 5.2.5)
The updated build displays the Apple Weather mark and a tappable "Weather data sources" legal link directly below the current conditions and hourly forecast in both portrait and landscape layouts. The marks and legal URL come from WeatherKit's attribution API. Weather is not displayed until the attribution is available.
To find it: allow location access, open the Ride tab and scroll to the weather card below the map. Rotate the phone to see the same attribution in the landscape weather card. Tap "Weather data sources" to open Apple's legal attribution page.
